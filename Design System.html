05 · Voice & copy
Storytelling, artisanal, never precious.
✓ DO
• "Pulled slow, served honest."
• "Chai that tastes like your grandmother's if you're lucky."
• "One cup is enough."
• Small, specific, human. Telugu when it fits naturally.
✗ DON'T
• "Experience artisanal specialty coffee."
• "Premium handcrafted beverages."
• "Curated selection of the finest beans."
• Jargon. Adjectives stacked like luggage. Corporate warmth.
